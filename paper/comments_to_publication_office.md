## Versión B — completa (~1700 caracteres)

Recomendada si EM no ofrece slot separado de Cover Letter — esta versión funciona como cover letter de facto.

```
Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript "Symbolic Regression as
Spectral Feature Engineering: Discovering Interpretable Indices for
Hydrothermal Alteration Mapping from Sentinel-2" for consideration in
ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing.

The work proposes symbolic regression as a feature-engineering
framework for multispectral classification, validated on hydrothermal
alteration mapping at three sites across two continents (Atacama and
Coquimbo, Chile; Cuprite, Nevada, USA) and with cross-domain
replication on land cover classification (central Germany). A central
contribution is the explicit distinction between formula
transferability (limited; site-specific) and method transferability
(robust across domains and validation schemes), supported by
polygon-disjoint and spatial block cross-validation, TOST equivalence
testing, and DeLong AUC tests.

We confirm that:
- The manuscript is original and has not been published elsewhere.
- It is not under consideration by any other journal.
- All co-authors have read and approved this submission.
- Code and discovered formulas are publicly available; data sources
  (Sentinel-2 L2A, SERNAGEOMIN, USGS, ESA WorldCover) are open access.
- We declare the use of Claude (Anthropic) for language editing,
  per the Declaration of Generative AI in the manuscript.

A Declaration of Interest Statement is uploaded separately; the
authors have no conflicts of interest to declare.

Thank you for considering our work.

Sincerely,
Francisco Parra O. (corresponding author)
Universidad de Santiago de Chile (USACH)
francisco.parra.o@usach.cl
```
